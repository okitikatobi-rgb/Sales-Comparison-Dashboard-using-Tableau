# Sales-Comparison-Dashboard-using-Tableau
 
**Project Overview** 

Sales Comparison Dashboard is an interactive Tableau dashboard designed to analyse and compare sales performance across different dimensions such as regions, products, time periods, and customer segments.  

This project helps stakeholders quickly identify top-performing products, underperforming regions, sales trends, and growth opportunities through clear visualisations and actionable insights. 
 
**Project Objective** 

To create an interactive dashboard for monitoring and comparing sales performance. 
Identify key sales drivers and trends across regions, categories, and time periods. 
Provide data-driven insights to support strategic decision-making in sales and marketing. 
Dataset 
Source: Superstore Sales Dataset

**Key Insights** 
 
Regional Performance Comparison: Significant differences exist between the two regions. For example, the west region outperforms the east in total sales by a wide margin. 

The West region contributes to most of the overall revenue, while the South region lags in sales volume but maintains competitive profitability in select categories. 

Clear variation in customer behaviour and product preferences was observed between the two regions, highlighting the need for region-specific sales and marketing strategies.
